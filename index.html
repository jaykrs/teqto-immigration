Frequently asked

Questions candidates usually have.

Do you guarantee a visa?

No. Visa, work-permit and residence decisions are made by the relevant government authorities. Teqto Immigration can provide guidance and application support, but cannot guarantee an approval.

Do you guarantee a job?

No. Job availability depends on genuine employer vacancies, candidate eligibility and selection. Candidates should verify employer and offer details before proceeding.

Can I apply for warehouse or factory jobs?

Yes, where genuine vacancies are available and the candidate meets the employer and immigration requirements of the destination country.

What documents will I need?

Requirements vary. Common documents may include a passport, CV, education or experience records, photographs and other country- or employer-specific documents.

Can I choose the country?

You can state your preference. The suitable route depends on your profile, current vacancies, employer requirements and immigration rules.

Are government fees included?

Not necessarily. Government, visa, medical, translation, travel and other third-party costs may apply separately. Fees should be clearly explained before any payment.
