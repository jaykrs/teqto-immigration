{name} FAQ

Common candidate questions.

Can Teqto guarantee a {name} visa?

No. Visa, work-permit and residence decisions are made by the relevant authorities. Teqto provides guidance and application support but cannot guarantee approval.

Is a job guaranteed?

No. Employment depends on genuine employer vacancies, selection and candidate eligibility. Verify employer and offer details before making payments.

Can I apply for warehouse or factory work?

Where genuine vacancies exist, candidates may explore relevant roles subject to employer requirements and the immigration rules applicable to the specific position.

What documents are normally required?

Requirements vary by route and employer. A passport, CV, employment or education records and other supporting documents may be required.

How long does the process take?

Processing times vary by employer, application type and government authority. No fixed timeline should be assumed without checking the applicable route.

Are third-party fees separate?

Government, visa, medical, translation, travel and other third-party charges may apply separately. All applicable costs should be explained before payment.
